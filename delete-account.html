Account and Data Deletion Request

At AI Coach, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support team at the email address listed on our Google Play Store page.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include the email address associated with your AI Coach account so we can locate your data.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete the following data from our servers:

  • Your authentication credentials (Email or Google Sign-In link).
  • Your basic profile information.
  • Your entire workout history, generated plans, and fitness goals.

What Data Is Kept?

We do not retain any of your personal data after the deletion process is complete. All associated records in our database are permanently erased.

Note: The deletion process is irreversible and typically completed within 7 business days of your request.
