Privacy Policy for AI Coach

Last updated: October 08, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the AI Coach app collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account (via email or Google Sign-In), we collect your email address and basic profile information.
  • Health and Exercise Data: The app collects your workout preferences, fitness goals, exercise history, and feedback on the workouts.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Generate and adapt your daily workouts using Artificial Intelligence.
  • Maintain a history of your gym progress.
  • Ensure the security and proper functioning of the app (cloud synchronization).

3. Data Sharing

AI Coach does not sell or rent your personal information. Your data is processed securely through reliable Google services (Firebase and Gemini API).

4. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures (including encrypted authentication and secure database rules) to protect your information against unauthorized access.

5. Data Deletion

You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Simply contact us through our support channels or use the in-app account deletion feature.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the support email provided on the Google Play Store.
